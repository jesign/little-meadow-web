# Little Meadow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Little Meadow ("the game") is a farming game made by Jesign ("we").

## Information we collect

None. Little Meadow does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

- The game does not require an account.
- The game does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
- The game does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or files.
- The game does not offer in-app purchases.

## Information stored on your device

Your farm (crops, animals, buildings, coins and progress) and your settings (for example sound and graphics
options) are saved only on your device so the game can continue where you left off. They are never sent to us.
Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Children

Little Meadow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that collect data (for example ads, in-app purchases, cloud saves or anonymous
usage statistics), we will update this policy and the game's store listing before that version is released. The
effective date above will change.
