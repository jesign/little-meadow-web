Everything a farm needs

Start with a small field and two hens. Grow it, season by season, into a busy country farm.

Grow through the seasons

Carrots, wheat, tomatoes, strawberries, pumpkins and more. Every crop has its favorite season, and each day brings its own weather.

Raise happy animals

Chickens, ducks, rabbits, sheep, cows, goats and pigs – with a loyal dog and cat. Feed them, pet them and breed your best.

Care for quality

Fertile soil, steady watering and busy bees make Good and Excellent harvests that sell for more and win at the County Fair.

Make and sell goods

Bake bread and cake, churn butter, press jam, spin yarn and jar honey. Fill orders and watch prices change with the seasons.

Build and decorate

Clear new land, raise barns and workshops, plant orchards and lay paths. A beautiful farm brings tourists who leave tips.

A year full of events

A planting festival, the County Fair, a harvest festival and a winter market – plus helpers to hire and a daily gift.

Take a look around

From your very first carrots to a farm the whole county visits.

Relaxed, on your terms

Little Meadow is made to unwind with. Play for a few minutes or an afternoon – your farm keeps growing gently in between, and nothing is ever lost.

  • No stress timersA game day passes in about 20 minutes.
  • Nothing diesCrops wait for you and animals stay safe while you're away.
  • Plays offlineNo account, no internet needed – your farm lives on your phone.
